Mengkai Hu 胡梦锴

PhD student · Power systems & intelligent modeling

I am a PhD student in Power Systems and Automation at Tsinghua University, advised by Prof. Chao Lu. Previously, I received my bachelor’s degree in Electrical Engineering and Automation from Hohai University.

My research focuses on power-system modeling and optimization, exploring how learning methods can help us understand and analyze dynamical systems. My earlier work spans electric vehicle–grid coordination, charging behavior, and renewable integration.
